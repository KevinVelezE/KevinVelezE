## Mathematical work

**Filtered Backprojection in Tomographic Reconstruction: A Theoretical and
Computational Study** — undergraduate thesis, supervised by Julio Delgado.
The work studies two-dimensional reconstruction through the Radon transform and
filtered backprojection, with numerical experiments on reconstruction filters
and sensitivity to noise.

**Filter Functions for Reconstruction Algorithms** — extended abstract and
conference presentation at **MAPI 4 (2026)**, coauthored with Juan Lopez.
The work explores Fourier multiplier symbols and their role in reconstruction.
